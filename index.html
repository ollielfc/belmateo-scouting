COACHING
SHAPES THE VIEW.

Working with players on the pitch has shaped what I look for on video: not just the action, but the decision before it, the movement around it and whether the player can repeat it.

Ollie Enock · Coach & Scout
Observe the details.
Understand the context.Belmateo Scouting · Bay Area

I coach across college, competitive club and high-school soccer. That work keeps me close to the day-to-day details of the game; how roles change from team to team, what a coach asks of a player, and which decisions make a difference.

Alongside coaching, I put together scouting reports and follow players across the college and professional game. I’m interested in recruitment that remains practical: what the player does well, where there are limits, and whether their strengths suit the level and role being considered. Belmateo Scouting is a place to collect my reports and make my thinking available to others working in the game.
